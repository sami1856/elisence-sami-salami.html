Metabolic & fitness journey

GLP-1, discipline, and long-term safety

The Elisence GLP-1 engine and fitness modules were shaped through structured founder journey work — combining structured dosing, activity, sleep, and mood tracking with strict non-diagnostic rules.

  • Significant, controlled weight loss without crash behaviour.
  • Daily tracking of energy, workouts and side-effects.
  • Used only as a safe, auditable data backbone – not a diagnostic tool.

Elisence never replaces doctors. It creates structured data and clean dashboards that families, clinicians and ministries can safely interpret together.
